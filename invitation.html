We work hard together every day, but we do not often get the chance to step away from the usual routine and simply enjoy being a team. This offsite is that chance. We will start by exploring how we grow and lead, reflect together, then put those ideas into practice through bowling and a cooking challenge. Expect some friendly competition, good food, a few surprises, and plenty of time to connect.

The Goal Have fun, get to know each other better, and come back as a stronger team.
