Bowling rules

🎳 Two games · one very serious spreadsheet
  • Each team bowls 2 full games on its assigned lane.
  • Combined two-game scores determine team rank: 30 / 20 / 10 / 5 points.
  • Each team nominates one representative for the Bonus Lane; representatives also bowl 2 games.
  • Bonus Lane rank gives the represented team ×1.5 / ×1.3 / ×1.1 / ×1.0.
