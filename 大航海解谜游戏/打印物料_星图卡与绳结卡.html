港口挑战 · 好望角 · 8 字结图卡

Port challenge · Cape of Good Hope · figure-eight knot card — 放在好望角港口桌上 / place on the Cape port table

绕过风暴角 Round the Cape

照图打一个 8 字结。Tie a figure-eight knot by following the pictures.

1

做一个圈：绳尾绕下来，从绳子上面压过去。

Make a loop: bring the tail round and cross it over the rope.

2

绳尾从绳子后面绕一圈。

Take the tail behind the rope, around its neck.

3

绳尾从前面穿进圈里，从下面出来。

Push the tail down through the loop from the front.

4

两头一起拉紧，看起来像数字 8。

Pull both ends tight: it looks like the number 8.

规则
船上每个人各拿一根绳，各打一个，限时 90 秒。
半数以上打成：2 金币；全员打成：3 金币。
Rules
Every sailor takes a rope and ties one knot, 90 seconds.
Half or more tied: 2 doubloons; everyone: 3.

给港务长：桌上再挂一个打好的样品结，比看图更直观。每条船来之前把绳子解开。短绳 6 根，每根约 60 cm，粗一点更好打。
For the Port Master: hang one finished sample knot beside the card; it helps more than the drawing. Untie the ropes between ships. Six short ropes, about 60 cm each; thicker rope is easier.

夜航星图 · 海豚号 Night chart · Dolphin

沿四角的裁切线剪下，放进海豚号的船长箱。四张拼在一起时，这张在左上。
Cut along the corner marks and put it in the Dolphin chest. When the four are joined, this card goes top-left.

建议用 200 g 以上的卡纸彩色打印，100% 比例。成品 15 × 15 cm，四张拼成 30 × 30 cm。
Print in colour on card stock (200 g+), at 100%. Each card is 15 × 15 cm; together they make 30 × 30 cm.

主持人参考 · 星图拼好后的样子（不要发给玩家）

Host reference · the joined chart (do not hand out)

答案 Answer：A（第五枚印章 · the fifth seal）。
四张卡的金色星星和虚线连成一个大写字母 A，罗盘玫瑰只有四张拼在中间时才完整。
The gold stars and dashed lines join into a capital A; the compass rose is only whole when all four cards meet at the centre.
