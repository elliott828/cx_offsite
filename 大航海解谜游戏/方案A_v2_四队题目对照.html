1–4 号铺住着 Pedro、Juan、Diego、Luis，职位是领航员、厨师、木匠、炮手（每人一个）。

  1. 领航员睡在 1 号铺。
  2. 厨师的铺号正好比 Juan 大 1。
  3. Luis 睡在最后一个铺位。
  4. Diego 既不是木匠，也不是厨师。
  5. 炮手和木匠的铺位不相邻。

厨师睡几号铺？这是第 2 位。

Pedro, Juan, Diego and Luis sleep in bunks 1–4 as navigator, cook, carpenter and gunner (one each).

  1. The navigator sleeps in bunk 1.
  2. The cook's bunk is exactly one higher than Juan's.
  3. Luis sleeps in the last bunk.
  4. Diego is neither the carpenter nor the cook.
  5. The gunner and the carpenter are not in adjacent bunks.

Which bunk is the cook in? That's digit 2.

答案Answer
3。Luis = 4 号；Diego 只能是领航员（1 号）；若 Juan = 3，木匠和炮手会相邻，矛盾；所以 Juan = 2，厨师 Pedro 在 3 号。
3. Luis = 4; Diego can only be the navigator (bunk 1); Juan = 3 would put carpenter and gunner side by side, so Juan = 2 and the cook Pedro is in bunk 3.

答案Answer

7: expected = contents + 5 kg barrel. Barrel 6 (rope) is 6 kg over, exactly the wet-rope note (1 kg × 6 coils); barrel 7 (5 sacks of rice) should be 30 kg but weighs 42 kg, 12 kg unexplained.
