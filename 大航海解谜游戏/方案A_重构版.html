方案 A 重构：五片航海图 v2Option A, rebuilt: The Five Fragments v2

保留测试里最受欢迎的部分：轻松、热闹、纯合作的主线。在这条主线上加更多谜题、更多跨队环节，再加一套金币计分，让"最佳团队"有据可依。对象是 4 支队伍、19 名玩家，组织者全部转成游戏里的角色。

Keeps what the playtest liked: light, noisy, cooperative. Adds more puzzles and more cross-team moments on top of that spine, plus doubloon scoring so the Winning Team award has a basis. Built for 4 Houses, 19 players, and organizers who become in-game characters.

测试结论与改动What the playtest showed, and what changes

问题 1 · 太快Issue 1 · Too fast

原版只有 3+1+2 道谜，提前完成后无事可做。

The original had only 3 + 1 + 2 puzzles; teams finished early with nothing left to do.

改法：Fix: 谜题总量约翻倍，难度不提高；增加可伸缩的"漂流瓶"支线来吸收快慢差异。Roughly double the puzzle count at the same difficulty; add elastic "message in a bottle" side quests that absorb speed differences.

问题 2 · 跨队只有一次Issue 2 · One cross-team moment

原版的跨队只有交换钥匙。

The only cross-team moment was swapping keys.

改法：Fix: 增加港口巡航（和组织者互动）、暴风雨抢答、星图拼合、漂流瓶争夺，加上原来的钥匙环，一共 5 种跨队/跨角色互动。Add the port circuit (with organizers), the storm quiz, the star-chart puzzle and the race for bottles; with the original key ring, that makes five kinds of cross-team or cross-role contact.

问题 3 · 人数变了Issue 3 · New headcount

5 队变 4 队，每队 3–5 人不等，组织者不参赛。

Five teams became four, with 3–5 players each; organizers don't play.

改法：Fix: 钥匙环改为 4 环；人少的队多发提示券；组织者分别担任舰队司令和 4 个港口的港务长。A four-link key ring; smaller teams get more hint tokens; the organizers become the Admiral and four Port Masters.

问题 4 · 没法评出赢家Issue 4 · No way to pick a winner

邀请函上有"Winning Team"奖，但原版纯合作，没有胜负。

The invitation promises a Winning Team award, but the original was purely cooperative.

改法：Fix: 主线仍是全舰队合作抵达，另设金币计分：速度、港口表现、支线、帮助他人都能得分。晚宴时公布。The main line stays cooperative; doubloons reward speed, port performance, side quests and helping others. Announced at dinner.

队伍、角色与故事Teams, roles and story

故事：1519 年，四艘船准备从里斯本出发寻找香料群岛。老船长临终前把航海图撕成四片，锁进四艘船的船长箱，每把钥匙都放在另一艘船上。今晚舰队在港口集结，要在开饭前抵达终点。终点是什么，最后才揭晓：四楼的 Flora 宴会厅。

Story: 1519. Four ships prepare to leave Lisbon for the Spice Islands. The dying Admiral tore the chart into four and locked one piece in each captain's chest, each key on a different ship. Tonight the fleet musters and must reach its destination before dinner. That destination is revealed only at the end: Banquet Flora on the fourth floor.

视频结尾台词建议："每一片地图都不完整。每一把钥匙，都不在它自己的船上。终点有一场盛宴在等你们。"

Suggested closing line for the video: "No fragment is complete. No key is on its own ship. And a feast waits at the end of the voyage."

B2–B5

港口巡航（和港务长互动）The port circuit (with the Port Masters)

NEW4 × 3′

吧台四角各设一个港口，由港务长把守。每条船先去自己的母港：到了要报出港口名作为口令，报不出就不能进港。之后按航线顺序绕一圈，四个港口都要去。每过一个港口，航行证上盖一个章，章上印着一个字母。

Each corner of the bar is a port guarded by a Port Master. A ship starts at its home port and must name it as the password to enter, then continues in route order until it has visited all four. Each port stamps the sailing pass; each stamp carries a letter.

Dolphin: L → C → G → MRose: C → G → M → LStar: G → M → L → CEagle: M → L → C → G

四条船从不同港口出发、顺序轮转，四个港口同一时间各接待一条船，基本不会排队。

Ships start at different ports and rotate in the same direction, so each port serves one ship at a time and queues are rare.

第三幕 · 夜航（34–44′）Act 3 · Night sailing (34–44′)

C1

暴风雨抢答Storm quiz

NEW5′

全体回到吧台中央，司令连读 8 题。每题念完后，谁先按下抢答按钮，就由他所在的船回答；答对得 1 金币，答错就轮到第二个按的船。不需要喊口号。

Everyone returns to the centre and the Admiral reads eight questions. After each one, the first person to hit a buzzer answers for their ship: right earns 1 doubloon, wrong passes it to the next ship to buzz. No chanting needed.

抢答按钮：可以买一套 4 键抢答器（会亮灯的那种），或者每条船用一部手机打开测试页的抢答按钮，司令那边会按服务器时间排出先后。

Buzzers: a cheap 4-button quiz buzzer set with lights works, or one phone per ship on the test page's buzz button; the Admiral's screen orders buzzes by server time.

C2

星图拼合（全舰队）Star-chart puzzle (whole fleet)

NEWFleet5′

司令宣布："乌云散了，但只有四张夜航星图拼在一起，才看得见方向。"每条船的船长箱里都有一张 15 × 15 cm 的星图卡，是一张古典星图的四分之一。四张按边缘拼成 30 × 30 cm 的整张星图：中间的罗盘玫瑰只有四张合在一起才完整，金色的星星和虚线连成一个大写字母 A，底部的铭牌写着"第五枚印章在星空中"。

The Admiral announces: "The clouds have parted, but only all four night charts together show the way." Each chest holds a 15 × 15 cm card, one quarter of an antique star chart. Joined edge to edge they make a 30 × 30 cm chart: the compass rose at the centre is only whole when all four meet, the gold stars and dashed lines draw a capital A, and the cartouche at the bottom reads "the fifth seal is in the stars".

用普通卡纸彩色打印即可，不需要透明胶片。四张卡的打印文件见《打印物料_星图卡与绳结卡》，每张卡的外角印着船名，拼的时候不会放错位置。

Ordinary card stock in colour, no transparency film. The print file is “打印物料_星图卡与绳结卡”; each card's outer corner carries its ship name, so the position is unambiguous.

终局（44–52′）Finale (44–52′)

D1

最终宝箱The final chest

6′

司令面前的宝箱挂着 5 位字母锁，盖子上写："按航线顺序盖上四个港口的印章，再加上星空给的第五枚。"每条船的航行证上都有四个章，但盖章的先后各不相同，要按航线 Lisbon → Cape → Goa → Malacca 重新排。

The chest before the Admiral has a 5-letter lock. The lid reads: "Stamp the four ports' seals in the order of the voyage, then add the fifth the stars gave you." Every pass holds four stamps, but in a different order per ship; they must be re-ordered by the route Lisbon → Cape → Goa → Malacca.

答案Answer

F · L · O · R · A = FLORA

宝箱里是老船长的信："我把地图撕成四片，因为只有四艘船一起，才到得了这里。香料群岛不在海图上，它在四楼。今晚的盛宴在 Flora，属于整支船队。"

Inside is the Admiral's letter: "I tore the chart into four because only four ships together could reach this place. The Spice Islands aren't on any chart. They're on the fourth floor. Tonight's feast is at Flora, and it belongs to the whole fleet."

谁先开箱都行，四条船都在场。开箱的船额外得 2 金币，44–52′ 内开箱则每条船各得 3 金币"全舰队抵达"奖。

Any ship may open it with everyone present. The opening ship earns 2 extra doubloons; if the chest opens before 52′, every ship earns 3 "fleet arrived" doubloons.

终点答案 FLORA 就是晚宴的宴会厅名，最后走去 4 楼 Flora 就是"靠岸"，游戏结局直接接上晚宴。

The final word is the banquet room itself, so the walk up to Flora on 4F is the landfall and the game flows straight into dinner.

漂流瓶支线（16–44′）Bottle side quests (16–44′)

在吧台区藏 6 个小漂流瓶（提前和酒吧确认可以放的位置），分两批放出：16′ 放 1–3 号，30′ 放 4–6 号，44′ 截止。每个瓶里一道小谜，答案交给司令；每个瓶只奖励第一个答对的船，得 1 金币。漂流瓶和第二、三幕同时进行，用来填等钥匙、等港口的空档。分两批是为了不让最快的船一次全部拿走。

Hide six small bottles around the bar (agree spots with the venue first) and release them in two waves: 1–3 at 16′, 4–6 at 30′, closing at 44′. Each holds a short riddle; answers go to the Admiral, and only the first ship to answer each bottle earns 1 doubloon. Bottles run alongside Acts 2 and 3 and fill the gaps while ships wait for keys or ports; two waves stop the fastest ship from sweeping all six.
